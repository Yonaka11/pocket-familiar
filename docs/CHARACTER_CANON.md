# Pocket Familiar Character Canon v1

## Core premise

Pocket Familiar's core cast is Seraphi Astrea and her three attendants: Emi, Kaelani, and Mira (EMK). Seraphi is the boss and a Seraphim Celestial Keeper whose human form has been lost. EMK help the player recover Seraphi's memories, restore balance between human and spirit nature, and ultimately return their boss to her true human form.

During the day, EMK can manifest their humanoid familiar forms. At night, their spirit-animal forms dominate. Each keeps the same personality across forms, but nighttime expression becomes more magical and creature-like.

## Seraphi Astrea

**Role:** Boss; Seraphim Celestial Keeper

**Base Spirit Form:** a compact cream-and-gold celestial familiar with six wings and a constantly orbiting halo. This is the mascot-facing form seen early in the game.

**Ascended Spirit Form:** Seraphi's level-up spirit form. She becomes larger and more radiant, her six wings fully express, sacred markings intensify, and the halo system becomes more elaborate.

**Human Restored Form:** Seraphi's true Seraphim form. This is a long-term story unlock rather than a starting character.

**Progression path:** Base Spirit -> Ascended Spirit -> Human Restored.

## Emi

**Day form:** Tech-Royal Familiar. Deep dark skin, black hair with yellow accents, amber/gold eyes, black/electric-yellow/cobalt visual identity. Bold, playful, spirited and touch-focused.

**Night spirit form:** Sparkborn Trickster. A fast dark spirit beast with long ears, bright amber eyes, electric cobalt-gold trails and a kinetic silhouette.

**Ascended night form:** stronger lightning, rhythm and circuit motifs, faster visual motion and brighter trails.

**Favorite expression:** play, music, boops, juggling, catches and trick throws.

## Kaelani

**Day form:** Graceful Bloom Familiar. Warm golden-brown skin, flowing dark hair, hazel-gold eyes, teal/bronze/earthy-gold styling. Warm, elegant, grounded and affectionate.

**Night spirit form:** Bloom Spirit. An elegant floral spirit beast with moonlit petals, soft gold light, teal accents and flowing ornament.

**Ascended night form:** richer petal trails, blessing effects and more celestial floral ornament.

**Favorite expression:** music, gifts, nighttime calm, gentle petting and graceful movement.

## Mira

**Day form:** Cozy Scholar Familiar. Petite scholarly silhouette, glasses, muted teal/charcoal/burgundy/brass styling. Thoughtful, sleepy, observant and comfort-seeking.

**Night spirit form:** Dreamwatch Scholar. A soft moonlit scholar beast with glasses, dream notes, lantern and book motifs.

**Ascended night form:** stronger star, archive, memory and dream effects.

**Favorite expression:** sleep, food, reading, quiet time, gifts and gentle petting.

## Seraphi restoration arc

The player should not be forced to prefer one activity type. Any core familiar can be fully leveled through normal play, while preferred activities only provide bonuses and character-specific reactions.

The first implementation target for unlocking Seraphi's restored human form is:

- Emi, Kaelani and Mira each reach Bond Level 10.
- Emi, Kaelani and Mira each reach Familiar Level 15.
- Three signature memories are recovered for each attendant.
- The shared Day and Night achievement chain is completed.

These thresholds are tuning values, not lore absolutes, and can be rebalanced after playtesting.

## Brand rule

Seraphi is the canonical Pocket Familiar representative and mascot. Emi, Kaelani and Mira are the flagship attendants and the emotional bridge between the player and Seraphi's restoration story.

The orbiting halo is Seraphi's signature visual motif. Her six wings identify her explicitly as a Seraphim in both spirit and restored human form.
