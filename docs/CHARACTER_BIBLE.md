# Pocket Familiar Character Bible v1

## Core premise

Pocket Familiar is built around a day/night dual-form cast. Emi, Kaelani, and Mira (EMK) are the three attendants of Seraphi Astrea, a Seraphim and the celestial boss of the group. Seraphi has lost her human form and begins the story sealed in a small spirit-familiar body.

The player strengthens bonds with EMK through normal play: touch interactions, gifts, walking, music, discoveries, achievements, and memories. No preferred activity is required for progression. Preferences only add flavor and modest bonus rewards.

As all three attendants grow, they restore Seraphi in stages:

1. **Base Spirit Seraphi** — the initial mascot/boss form.
2. **Ascended Seraphim Spirit** — unlocked after meaningful progress with all three attendants.
3. **Restored Human Seraphi** — the long-term story unlock after all three attendants reach the required bond, play, achievement, and memory milestones.

## World rule: day and night forms

EMK keep the same core identity in both forms.

- **Day:** humanoid/chibi familiar form. More social, expressive, gift-oriented, and directly interactive.
- **Night:** anime spirit-animal form. More magical, ambient, and personality-driven.

The form shift should feel like two expressions of the same character, not two separate characters.

## Seraphi Astrea

**Role:** Boss / Celestial Keeper  
**Species:** Seraphim  
**Core:** ancient, kind, regal, protective, luminous

### Base spirit form

A compact cream-white celestial beast with golden eyes, a constantly orbiting halo, sacred markings, and a restrained six-wing motif. This is the app's primary mascot form and the form Seraphi is trapped in at the start of the story.

### Ascended spirit form

A larger, radiant level-up form. All six wings fully manifest, halo geometry becomes more complex, and her markings intensify. This is a real progression state, not only a cosmetic swap.

Suggested signature perk: **Halo Blessing**, a periodic bonus to Bond XP or Charm gain for the active familiar.

### Restored human form

Seraphi's true form: a celestial Seraphim keeper with six angelic wings, ivory-and-gold regalia, golden eyes, halo-crown geometry, and a celestial staff. This is the major story reward for progressing the whole cast.

## Emi

**Role:** Playful Tech-Royal Attendant  
**Core:** playful, bold, spirited, teasing, attention-loving

### Day form

A very dark-skinned chibi humanoid with amber-gold eyes, a black bob with electric-yellow accents, and a black/yellow/cobalt/chrome visual language.

**Favorite themes:** play, music, touch interactions  
**Best interactions:** boop, double tap, juggle, catch, trick throw

### Night spirit form: Sparkborn Trickster

A fast cat/fox/rabbit-like dark spirit with amber eyes, electric cobalt streaks, a gold crest, and a fast luminous halo trail. It should feel kinetic even while idle.

Unique nighttime flavor: zoomies, light chasing, rhythm reactions, smug catch poses, playful pounces.

## Kaelani

**Role:** Graceful Bloom Attendant  
**Core:** graceful, warm, grounded, affectionate, composed

### Day form

A warm golden-brown chibi humanoid with hazel-gold eyes, flowing dark curls, and a teal/bronze/earthy-gold palette.

**Favorite themes:** music, gifts, night/calm  
**Best interactions:** pet, tap, gifts, gentle holds, music mode

### Night spirit form: Bloom Spirit

An elegant deer/cat/fox-like moon spirit with floral antler or ear accents, teal ribbons, golden petals, and a moonlit bloom halo.

Unique nighttime flavor: flower trails, soft humming, graceful twirls, petal memories, cozy nesting.

## Mira

**Role:** Cozy Scholar Attendant  
**Core:** thoughtful, shy, sleepy, sweet, comfort-seeking

### Day form

A petite scholarly chibi humanoid with glasses and a muted teal/charcoal/burgundy/antique-brass palette.

**Favorite themes:** sleep, food, reading, quiet time  
**Best interactions:** pet, tap, gifts, sleep-time interactions

### Night spirit form: Dreamwatch Scholar

A rounded cat/owl/rabbit-like dream spirit with soft gray-teal fur, round glasses, a moon halo, and book/lantern motifs.

Unique nighttime flavor: reading glowing books, collecting dream fragments, snack cuddles, curled-up moonlit sleep.

## Progression philosophy

- Every familiar can reach max level without using their preferred activity.
- Preferences affect bonus XP, reactions, memories, and personality flavor only.
- Bond XP is universal relationship progression.
- Play XP tracks touch-interaction mastery.
- Charms are earned through activity and can be spent on gifts.
- Gifts always help; favorites simply give a stronger reaction or bonus.
- Achievements and memories should reward experimentation, not grinding.

## Seraphi restoration milestones

Initial tuning target:

### Ascended spirit unlock

All three attendants:
- Bond Level 5+
- At least 1 achievement each

### Restored human unlock

All three attendants:
- Bond Level 10+
- Play Level 5+
- At least 3 achievements each
- At least 3 recovered memories each

These numbers are tuning defaults, not sacred values. The important rule is that Seraphi's restoration requires meaningful progress with **all three** attendants.

## Art direction

Pocket Familiar uses sharp, readable anime/chibi forms with strong silhouettes and expressive faces. Designs should remain readable at roughly 48–128 px in overlay mode.

Spirit forms should be original creature designs. Inspiration can come from the broad language of magical-anime familiars, cabbits, celestial beasts, and mascot creatures, but final silhouettes, markings, accessories, and lore must remain distinctive to Pocket Familiar.

## Canon lock

The following concepts are considered locked for the current Pocket Familiar canon:

- The product name is **Pocket Familiar**.
- Seraphi Astrea is the boss of EMK.
- Seraphi Astrea is a **Seraphim**.
- Seraphi begins trapped in a spirit-familiar form.
- Emi, Kaelani, and Mira are helping restore her human form.
- EMK have humanoid day forms and spirit-animal night forms.
- Seraphi has Base Spirit, Ascended Spirit, and Restored Human progression stages.
- Progress with all three attendants is required to restore Seraphi's human form.
- Preferences are bonuses, never progression gates.
